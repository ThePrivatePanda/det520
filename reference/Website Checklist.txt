1.  [Yes] Website, to include videos and background music, complies with copyright restrictions.
    Copyrighted material may only be used with written permission from the owner. (5.8.3.5.)

2.  [Yes] Website contains accurate, current information and links. All hyperlinks are recently
    validated. Recommend the inclusion of a "Current as of" date. (AFI 35-101, Attach 2, #10)
    CAO: 13 June 2023

3.  [Yes] Homepage contains links to www.af.mil, www.spaceforce.mil, www.airforce.com, the Holm
    Center, (www.airuniversity.af.edu/Holm-Center), AFROTC Recruiting, (www.afrotc.com) and the
    AF FOIA site, (www.compliance.af.mil). Pages do not link to information outside functional
    area or mission. (5.4.3.1.) (5.8.1.4.) (5.8.2.1.) (AFI 35-101, Attach 2, #10)

4.  [Yes] Pages are not used to promote personal/commercial gain or endorse commercial products
    or services. (5.8.2.2.)

5.  [Yes] Pages do not display incomplete paths, "access denied." or "under construction"
    messages. (AFI 35-101, Attach 2, #10)

6.  [Yes] Homepage/subordinate pages display required warning notices and banners (Privacy and
    Security Notice and External Disclaimer for outside and non-government links).
    (5.4.3.5.) (5.8.2.5.)

7.  [Yes] Each unit or organizational top-level page contains organization name and shield,
    organization office symbol, commercial telephone number, organizational email, and/or
    feedback form. (5.8.1.6.)

8.  [Yes] Webpage does not provide a list of names or individual e-mail addresses that are exempt
    from release under FOIA. (Note: one e-mail address required for legitimate inquiries or page
    maintenance is acceptable; however, it must be organizational/generic.) (5.8.1.6.)

9.  [Yes] Pages do not contain information intended only for military members, cadre, or current
    cadets. Cadet pages are password protected. (5.8.3.2. and 5.8.3.3.)

10. [Yes] Pages comply with the Privacy Act. Pages do not contain personally identifying
    information to include personal or business emails, phone numbers, SSNs, and birth dates.
    There are no lists of names or e-mail addresses on rosters, telephone directories, or
    organizational charts. (5.8.3.4.)

11. [Yes] The only biographies/names posted on the site are those of Air Force officers, enlisted
    and staff civilians who interact with the general public (recruiting purposes). There are no
    biographies or lists of cadets posted. (Biographies reveal official duty information only;
    they do not reveal birth place of the member, family members' names, or other personally
    identifying information.). (5.8.1.2.)

12. [Yes] Pages do not contain Classified, CUI or FOUO information (information exempt from release
    under the FOIA according to DoD 5400.7R/AF Supplement 1), (5.8.3.3.).

13. [Yes] Pages do not contain AFI 10-701 critical information (OPSEC) or sensitive mission data
    that by itself is unclassified, but when combined with other available data may reveal
    classified information. (AFMAN 35-101, Para. 8.5.1.)

14. [Yes] Appearance of website is professional. Subordinate pages are recognizable as part of the
    overall site. Pages load quickly and are not hampered by large graphics. Graphics, animations,
    and sound files are limited and purposeful and do not detract from the overall quality of the
    website. Navigation is simple, intuitive, and consistent throughout site. (5.8.3.1.) (5.8.3.3.)

15. [Yes] Website resides on the host institution (.edu) webserver. (5.4.6.)

16. [Yes] No links to the university donation page, nor any solicitation for donations on the site.
    Solicitation by a federal entity, to include AFROTC detachments, is prohibited by the Joint
    Ethics Regulation and Holm Center Instruction 51-601, paragraph 1.2.2.

17. [Yes] Website does not require or encourage users to choose any specific browser software for
    viewing. (5.8.2.4)

18. [Yes] All videos uploaded to the Website include transcripts or closed captioning to comply
    with Section 50[8] usability requirements for people with disabilities. (5.9.2.)

19. [Yes] All documents are loaded as "native content" with no format or in PDF format for public
    display on Websites. (5.4.5.)
