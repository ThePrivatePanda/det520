TSgt Jalen J.A. Gayle is an Officer Accessions Instructor at Detachment 520, Cornell University, Ithaca, New York. In this capacity, he serves as an instructor and primary enlisted advisor on cadet academics, professional development, military discipline, and welfare for over 60 United States Air Force and United States Space Force officer candidates. Additionally, he is directly responsible for all cadet life-cycle personnel actions, including in-processing, medical, physical, field training, enlistments, categorization, and commissioning. He also oversees all cadet pay actions including tuition, fees, subsistence, and textbook funds in excess of $2.5M annually.

TSgt Gayle enlisted in the Air Force in January 2018. After completing Basic Military Training, he graduated structures technical school at Gulfport Naval base August 2018. Prior to his current position, Sergeant Gayle was the Structures Craft Lead, 819th RED HORSE Squadron, Malmstrom AFB, Montana.

After graduation from technical training, TSgt Gayle’s was assigned to Misawa AB as a Structural Apprentice. During this period, he was assigned to Snow Removal Operations in 2019 and was a part of the 35th CES unit that won the #1 best snow team in the Air Force. Following Misawa AB, TSgt Gayle PCS’d to Malmstrom AFB and became a structural supervisor and craft lead for a Large Vertical Construction team. During this assignment, he helped fabricate a $1.98M Unit Assembly Area facility for the 891th RED HORSE Squadron. His efforts contributed to his team winning the General Mark A. Welsh III “One Air Force” Award.
