OFFICIAL BIOGRAPHY // UNITED STATES AIR FORCE

John J. Novotny

Major, United States Air Force — Assistant Professor of Aerospace Studies & Recruiting Officer, AFROTC Detachment 520, Cornell University.

(607) 255-8503
113 Barton Hall, Ithaca, NY

← All cadre

Maj John Novotny is an Assistant Professor of Aerospace Studies and Recruiting Officer, Detachment 520, Cornell University, New York. He leads and instructs 66 cadets in the completion and certification of all requirements required to commission into the United States Air Force.

Maj Novotny is a prior-enlisted career intelligence officer. After enlisting and serving as an airborne cryptologic linguist for 7 years, he received his commission in 2015 through Officer Training School at Maxwell Air Force Base, Alabama. He completed intelligence officer training in July 2016, and since then has held multiple intelligence positions. From 2016-2020, Maj Novotny served as a course director, instructor, and evaluator for two intelligence formal training courses at the 423rd Mobility Training Squadron, United States Air Force Expeditionary Operations School, Joint Base McGuire-Dix-Lakehurst. Additionally, from January to May 2019, he served as Officer in Charge of Intelligence support to the 340th Expeditionary Air Refueling Squadron while deployed to Al-Udeid Air Base, Qatar.

Prior to his current position, Maj Novotny was the Deputy Chief, Analysis, Correlation, and Fusion at 613th Air Operations Center, Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam, Hawaii.  During this time, he led a team of 17 intel analysts providing the 613th Air Operations Center and 59 tactical units with timely and tailored all-source intelligence, which supported the planning, execution, and assessment of theater wide air operations in line with Theater Joint Force Air Component Commander strategic objectives.
