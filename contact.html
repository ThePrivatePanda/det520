02 // VISITING

Parking at Barton Hall

Short-term permits

Available at any information and parking booth (Mon–Fri, 7:30 AM – 5:00 PM) or the parking office at 116 Maple Avenue (7:30 AM – 4:00 PM). All-day $10, four-hour $6.

Loading & metered

A complimentary 30-minute loading permit can be requested at the booth. Metered parking runs about $1.50 an hour at most locations.
