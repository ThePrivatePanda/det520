Samantha M. Ehlers

SSgt Samantha M. Ehlers is the Non-Commissioned Officer in Charge of Administration Management at Detachment 520, Cornell University, Ithaca, New York. Responsibilities include overseeing the cadet life-cycle, while supporting cadre and over 50 cadets. As well as completing actions for contracting, commissioning, enlistments, scholarships, Extended Active Duty, Air Force Reserve Officer Training Corps Field Training, and other events/programs.

SSgt Ehlers entered the Air Force in February 2017. After completing Basic Military Training, she graduated Personnel technical school at Keesler AFB, Mississippi in May 2017 and since then has held a variety of Personnel positions at the Military Personnel Flight and in Commander Support Staff offices. Prior to her current position, Sergeant Ehlers was the NCOIC of the Medical Group CSS, Shaw AFB, South Carolina.

After graduation, SSgt Ehlers’ first duty station was Barksdale AFB, Louisiana and she began as a Career Development Apprentice within the Retentions office for the 2nd FSS, Barksdale AFB, Louisiana. Soon after, she experienced her very first CSS in the 2nd Security Forces Squadron, Barksdale AFB, Louisiana and her very first deployment to Ali Al Salem AB, Kuwait. Taking on Reception, Staging, Onward Movement, and Integration (RSO&I). During this period, as an A1C she was in charge of meeting all inbound Personnel to ensure more than 400 Airman serving in 24 locations across 14 countries were properly accounted for, executing 1,200 weapon, ammunition, equipment, and medical tasks for the second largest squadron in Air Force’s Central Command.
